About us

Making scholarships easier to find

ScholarMate AI was built for students who lose out on scholarships not because they don't qualify, but because the right one is hard to find. We turn plain descriptions of your background into ranked, explained matches — no forms full of jargon, no digging through PDFs.

Our mission

Close the gap between students and the funding they're already eligible for.

How we help

Natural-language matching plus plain-English eligibility explanations for every result.

Who it serves

Students across state and central education boards seeking verified scholarship opportunities without bureaucratic friction.
